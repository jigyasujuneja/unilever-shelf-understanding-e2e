# HUL Training Data Audit and Architecture Sign-Off (10-Minute Client Readout)

## 1. Context and Objective (1 Minute)

We audited all `1,002,209` unzipped training crops (`602,440` unique MD5 hashes) across all `2,091` folders in `gs://marketshare-datasets`:
* **`brand_data/brand/brand_data/`**: `941` folders (`522,102` crops)
* **`input_data/variant_*/train/`**: `1,104` folders (`381,619` crops across `variant_skin` `438`, `variant_p5` `291`, `variant_wash` `173`, `variant_gen` `145`, and `variant_oral` `57`)
* **`pack_data/pack/train/`**: `46` `<Category>__<PackagingType>` folders (`98,488` crops across `10` categories and `14` container types)

Our goal for this 10-minute review is to share what we found across the unzipped folders and raw images, show how our pipeline handles these patterns automatically today, and get your sign-off on **three taxonomy and scope decisions** before we lock the production reference catalog in Cloud SQL.

### B. Raw Pixel Quality Audit on Live Client Crops (`gemini-embedding-2` vs. Visual Limits)

We measured resolution, aspect ratio, edge sharpness (Laplacian gradient variance), and specular glare across live client crops in `gs://marketshare-datasets`. `gemini-embedding-2` handles clean crops (`>= 64px`) at `100%` accuracy with zero VLM calls, but raw pixel inspection exposed **four physical failure modes** where embedding vectors alone are not enough:

| Pixel Quality Issue | Measured Rate in Live GCS Crops | Why `gemini-embedding-2` Alone Fails | How Our Pipeline Solves It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Ultra-low resolution crops (`< 64px` short side)** | **`66.1%`** of `brand_data` crops (`median = 48px`, down to `22x88px`); **`11.5%`** of `variant_*` crops (`down to 24px`) | Bilinear upscaling into the embedding encoder blurs micro-typography; standalone embedding retrieval drops to `0.850` to `0.900` accuracy on `brand_data`. | **Z-Score + Margin Gate** detects low-margin crops (`z1 < 2.2`) and routes them to a batched `gemini-3.8-flash` / `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` contact sheet (`1.000` accuracy). |
| **2. Motion blur and defocus** | **`35.5%`** of `pack_data` crops and **`17.3%`** of `variant_*` crops have low edge variance (`< 150`) | Blurred text on sachets and cartons collapses fine variant embeddings toward the brand centroid. | **2-Stage Hierarchical Routing** locks the Brand first (`0.98` to `1.00` accuracy), then restricts variant search to the `8` to `25` SKUs inside that brand. |
| **3. Extreme aspect ratios (`> 3:1`, up to `5.17:1`) and size normalization** | **`20.3%`** of `brand_data` and **`17.3%`** of `variant_*` crops exceed `3:1` (`489x115px` toothbrushes, `26x105px` vertical strips) | Resizing isolated crops to a square frame destroys physical scale (`100g` vs `250g` `Closeup` have `0.994` cosine similarity). | **Aspect-Ratio Prior** penalizes mismatched shapes (`w/h`), and **Bottom-30% Zoom Strips** preserve fine net-weight text on the contact sheet. |
| **4. Specular foil glare and store lighting cast** | **`5.1%` to `7.7%`** of crops have `> 5%` saturated glare pixels (`RGB > 245`) on metallic pouches (`Surf_Excel`, `Rin`, `Bru`) | Specular highlights and warm kirana tungsten lighting shift raw RGB color histograms across stores. | **Stage 0 Minkowski ($p=6$) White Balance** and **Inner-80% 3-Zone CIELAB** strip glare and background shelf bleed before scoring. |

## 3. How We Manage and Process This Today (2 Minutes)

We built an automated data and inference pipeline in [`src/utils/hul_taxonomy.py`](../src/utils/hul_taxonomy.py) and [`src/utils/hul_builder.py`](../src/utils/hul_builder.py) that handles these patterns without manual cleanup:

1. **Exact MD5 Cross-Silo Join & Synthetic Copy Quarantine**: We join `pack_data`, `variant_*`, and `brand_data` strictly by exact `md5_hash` (linking `45,108` `pack_data` crops to exact variants and `21,816` to brands), strip all `copy*`, `(copy)`, `0 (2).jpg`, and `DA_TBC_*` synthetic files (`8,398` in `pack_data`), and normalize prefixes (`DA_TBC_100_10.jpg -> session 100`) so store sessions never leak between `train`, `val`, and `test`.
2. **Multi-Folder Conflict Quarantine & Taxonomy Backfill**: We quarantine all `57` intra-`pack_data` and `497` intra-`variant_*` conflicted MD5 hashes from gallery and evaluation splits, keep `Dove` and `Ayush` defaulted to `Skin` at the brand level while overriding only the specific misplaced `Dove` hair folders (`Shampoo`, `Conditioner`, `Conditionar`, `Mask` -> `Hair_Care`), `Dove` personal-wash folders (`Bathing_Bar`, `Body_Wash` -> `PW`), and `Ayush` oral folders (`Toothpaste`, `Mouthwash` -> `Oral_Care`) in `variant_skin`, and backfill `packaging_type` across all `781` suffix-free variant folders via MD5 linkage + product-form rules + our `clm_standalone` pack head (`95.0%` accuracy).
3. **2-Stage Hierarchical Routing on Cloud SQL (`pgvector` HNSW)**: At inference time, Stage 1 matches the crop against brand centroids (`1.84 ms` p50 in Cloud SQL). Stage 2 restricts variant retrieval to the top-2 brands (`~20` candidates instead of `1,104`), resolving `75%` to `85%` of boxes with zero VLM calls and sending only ambiguous or promo crops to `gemini-3.8-flash` (`₹0.011` to `₹0.046` per image with Priority PayGo `1.8x` and the 50% Flash promotional credit).

## 4. Three Client Questions and Sign-Offs (4 Minutes)

### Question 1: `pack_data` Container Boundaries (`57` MD5 Conflicts) and Synthetic Oversampling (`8,398` Crops)

* **What we found**: Inside `pack_data/pack/train/` (`46` folders, `98,488` crops), `57` byte-identical MD5 hashes appear in two different folders (`HFD__Bottle` vs `HFD__Jar` on `Horlicks`, `HFD__Pouch` vs `HFD__Sachet` on `Boost__3x_Poly` vs `_Rs_5`, `DMT__Bottle` vs `DMT__Can`, and `Hair__Tube` vs `Skin__Tube`). In addition, rare pack folders (`Oral_Care__Can` `78%`, `Beverages__Tin` `66%`, `Laundry__Can` `57%`, `Laundry__Carton` `53%`, `Laundry__Sachet` `50%`) are padded with `8,398` synthetic `copy*.jpg` and `DA_TBC_*` augmented files.
* **How we handle it today**: We automatically quarantine all `8,398` synthetic copies and `57` conflicting MD5 hashes before building the Cloud SQL reference gallery and evaluation splits.
* **Sign-off needed**:
  1. What is Unilever's official catalog rule separating `Bottle` vs `Jar` in HFD (`Horlicks` wide-mouth plastic container) and `Pouch` vs `Sachet` (is `Sachet` defined by price point `<= Rs 10` / single-use grammage or by a hanger strip)?
  2. Do you approve stripping all `copy*`, `(copy)`, and `DA_TBC_*` synthetic files from `pack_data` and `variant_*` so evaluation splits measure true store generalization?

### Question 2: Intra-Variant Twin Folders (`497` MD5 Conflicts), Grammage Scope, and Full-Shelf Coordinates

* **What we found**: Across the `1,104` `variant_*` folders, `497` byte-identical MD5 hashes sit in two folders simultaneously (for example, `Dove__Dryness_Care__Conditioner` vs `Dove__Dryness_Care__Conditioner_Tube`, `Rin__Liquid_Detergent_Bottle` vs `Rin__Liquid_TL_Bottle`, and `Closeup__Fresh_Breath` vs `_Mega`). Furthermore, while `652` files in `brand_data` include `1920x1080` shelf bounding boxes (`55.01____HUL-...__43__883_352_941_409.jpg`), the `381,619` `variant_*` crops and `98,488` `pack_data` crops are isolated cutouts where `100g` and `250g` packs share `0.994` visual cosine similarity.
* **How we handle it today**: We merge suffix-free twin folders into their canonical container folder, quarantine the remaining conflicted MD5s, and evaluate isolated crops at the visual `base_variant` level (`Brand + Variant + Packaging Type`) while using bounding-box pixel dimensions `(w, h)` and bottom-30% zoom strips on full-shelf images.
* **Sign-off needed**:
  1. Do you approve merging suffix-free twin folders (`Dove__Dryness_Care__Conditioner` + `_Tube`) and scoring isolated crops at the visual variant level (`Brand + Variant + Packaging Type`)?
  2. Can your data engineering team export the uncropped full-shelf bay images and bounding-box coordinate tables for the `340xxxx` and `HUL-*` store sessions so we can benchmark end-to-end detection, grammage size, and planogram compliance on full kirana bays?

### Question 3: Promotional Pack Hierarchy (`69` Promo Folders) and Misplaced Category Folders (`21` Folders)

* **What we found**: `69` variant folders encode promotional, multipack, or price-point callouts in the folder name (`Red_Label__Natural_Care_Poly_Rs_10`, `Boost__3x_Poly_Rs_5`, `Closeup__Everfresh_Redhot_B3G1_Toothpaste`, `Closeup__Everfresh_Redhot_PO2_Toothpaste`) and share identical base artwork (`497` shared MD5s) with their parent SKU folder. Separately, `12` `Dove` hair-conditioner folders and `9` `Ayush` toothpaste/mouthwash folders sit inside `variant_skin` instead of `variant_gen` and `variant_oral`, and `3` HUL brands (`Axe`, `Brut`, `ABrook_Bond`) exist in `brand_data` with zero folders in `variant_*`.
* **How we handle it today**: We decompose promo folders into `base_variant` (counted toward Track 1 Market Share / SOS) + `promo_type` (`B3G1`, `PO2`, `RS10`, verified by `jev_promo_verifier` at `95%` to `100%` accuracy in Track 2), keep `Dove` and `Ayush` mapped to `Skin` by default while routing only the specific misplaced `Dove` hair folders (`Hair_Care`) and `Ayush` oral folders (`Oral_Care`) via product-form keywords and `pack_data` MD5 consensus, and score `Axe`, `Brut`, and `Brooke_Bond` at the `W3` Brand level.
* **Sign-off needed**:
  1. Do you approve rolling promotional and price-point facings (`_B3G1`, `_PO2`, `_Rs_10`) into their parent `base_variant` for Market Share (Track 1) while tracking `promo_type` as a separate Merchandising Compliance attribute (Track 2)?
  2. Do you approve our automated category overrides for the `21` specific `Dove` hair and `Ayush` oral folders in `variant_skin`, and scoring `Axe`, `Brut`, and `Brooke_Bond` at the Brand level until variant trees are provided?
