# Unilever (HUL) Data Hygiene, Cross-Silo Linkage & SME Sign-Off Readout

## 1. Audit Scope (`1,002,209` Crops Across `2,091` Unzipped Folders)

We ran an uncapped full-bucket audit across all `1,002,209` image files (`602,440` unique MD5 hashes) in the three unzipped trees of `gs://marketshare-datasets`:
1. **`brand_data/brand/brand_data/`**: `941` raw folders (`934` canonical brands after merging `7` `A`-prefix twin pairs and mapping cross-silo aliases), `522,102` crops.
2. **`input_data/variant_*/train/`**: `1,104` variant folders (`381,619` crops) across five unzipped trees:
   * `variant_skin/train/`: `438` folders (`136,286` crops)
   * `variant_wash/train/`: `173` folders (`106,649` crops)
   * `variant_p5/train/` (HFD, Beverages, Foods, HHC): `291` folders (`83,053` crops)
   * `variant_gen/train/` (Hair Care & Deodorants): `145` folders (`42,486` crops)
   * `variant_oral/train/` (Oral Care): `57` folders (`13,145` crops)
3. **`pack_data/pack/train/`**: `46` `<Category>__<PackagingType>` folders, `98,488` crops across `10` categories (`Skin`: `17,227`, `DMT`: `16,022`, `Laundry`: `13,484`, `Hair`: `11,020`, `PW`: `9,887`, `Beverages`: `8,853`, `Oral_Care`: `7,421`, `HFD`: `5,481`, `Foods`: `5,445`, `HHC`: `3,648`) and `14` packaging types (`Bottle`: `24,834`, `Tube`: `17,911`, `Can`: `11,315`, `Pouch`: `10,613`, `Jar`: `8,457`, `Wrapper`: `7,844`, `Carton`: `6,923`, `Bar`: `4,180`, `Sachet`: `3,866`, `Tetrapack`: `1,276`, `Tub`: `558`, `Tin`: `369`, `Pump`: `216`, `Box`: `126`).

## 3. Client SME Sign-Off Questions

1. **Container Boundary Definitions in `pack_data` (`57` MD5 Conflicts)**: Byte-identical crops appear in both `HFD__Bottle` and `HFD__Jar` (`Horlicks`), `HFD__Pouch` and `HFD__Sachet` (`Boost__3x_Poly` vs `_Rs_5`), `DMT__Bottle` and `DMT__Can`, and `Hair__Tube` and `Skin__Tube`. What is Unilever's official rule separating `Bottle` vs `Jar` in HFD (wide-mouth plastic container) and `Pouch` vs `Sachet` (grammage/price threshold such as `Rs 5`/`Rs 10`, or physical hanger strip)?
2. **In-Place Synthetic Oversampling in `pack_data` (`8,398` `copy*` / `DA_TBC_*` Crops)**: Over `50%` of crops in rare packaging folders (`Oral_Care__Can`, `Beverages__Tin`, `Laundry__Can`, `Laundry__Carton`, `Laundry__Sachet`) are `copy*.jpg` duplicates, and `Skin__Tube` / `Skin__Bottle` contain `2,724` `DA_TBC_*` jittered crops (`117x387` crop of `131x387` original). Do you approve stripping all synthetic copies (`copy*`, `(copy)`, `DA_TBC_*`) before building the Cloud SQL reference gallery and evaluation splits?
3. **Cross-Category Folder Misplacements Across `variant_*`**: Because folders were grouped by brand into a single `variant_*` tree, `47` `Dove` hair-care folders (`Shampoo`, `Conditioner`, `Conditionar`, `Mask`, `Beautiful_Curls`, `Mencare_2_In_1`) and `8` `Ayush` toothpaste/hair folders sit inside `variant_skin` (while their byte-identical MD5 matches in `pack_data` are labeled `Hair` and `Oral_Care`), and `Lbp` bodylotions/bodywashes sit inside `variant_gen`. We store both `raw_silo_category` (`gen, oral, p5, skin, wash`) and the `pack_data`-aligned 10-category `category`: please confirm which category schema downstream consumers require.
4. **Intra-Variant Twins & Byte-Identical Crop Conflicts (`497` MD5s)**: Byte-identical crops sit in both suffix and suffix-free folders (`Dove__Dryness_Care__Conditioner` vs `_Conditioner_Tube`, `Rin__Liquid_Detergent_Bottle` vs `Rin__Liquid_TL_Bottle`) and in `Closeup__Fresh_Breath` vs `_Mega`. Do you approve merging suffix-free twin folders into their canonical container folder and evaluating size-identical crops at the `base_variant` (`Brand + Variant + Packaging Type`) level when full-shelf bounding boxes are absent?
5. **Promotional, Multipack & Price-Point Hierarchy (`69` Folders)**: Folders such as `Red_Label__Natural_Care_Poly_Rs_10`, `Boost__3x_Poly_Rs_5`, `Closeup__Everfresh_Redhot_B3G1_Toothpaste`, and `Closeup__Everfresh_Redhot_PO2_Toothpaste` share identical base artwork with their parent SKU. Should these facings roll up into `base_variant` for Market Share (Track 1) while being scored separately on `promo_type` (`B3G1`, `PO2`, `RS10`) for Merchandising Compliance (Track 2)?
6. **Uncropped Full-Shelf Bay Images & Detector Coordinate Tables**: While `brand_data` contains `652` filenames with full-shelf `1920x1080` coordinates (`55.01____HUL-...__43__883_352_941_409.jpg`), the `381,619` `variant_*` crops and `98,488` `pack_data` crops are pre-cutout crops without full-shelf images. Can your data engineering team export the uncropped full-shelf bay images and bounding-box coordinate CSVs for the `340xxxx` / `HUL-*` store sessions?
