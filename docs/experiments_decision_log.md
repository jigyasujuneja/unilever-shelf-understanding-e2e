## 1. Executive Summary of Our Experimental Journey

Rather than guessing which model or architecture works best for Unilever's `506,000` daily shelf photos (`MT MarketShare`, `MT & GT Merchandising`, `Toker Promo`, and `SOS & P2P`), we executed **19 controlled experiments (`EXP-01` to `EXP-19`)** across `63` Cloud Run benchmark runs (`gs://ul-fs-s-sandbx-16-prj-shelf-images/results/`) and designed a **6-step sequential Phase-2 experiment ladder (`EXP-20` to `EXP-25`)** to close the remaining gaps on squeeze tubes (`77.0%`), soap bars (`83.6%`), and exact grammage (`50g` vs `100g`).

### Key Learnings That Shaped Our Architecture
1. **Why Single-Call Multimodal LLMs Fail on Full Shelves (`EXP-01`, `EXP-11`):** Asking a single Gemini call to both draw bounding boxes and identify 40+ packs on a dense shelf achieved only `51.0%–71.3% F2` and missed `30%+` of small packs because vision-language token grids lack spatial precision on crowded shelves. **Decision:** Always use a dedicated **Stage 1 spatial detector (`yolo11n-distilled-onnx`)** first (`94.7% F2` at `80ms` CPU, `₹0`).
2. **Why Raw Client Folders Had a `32%–57%` Ceiling (`Data Audit` before `EXP-05`):** Auditing `1,002,209` crops across `2,091` folders and `53,019` client JSONs revealed `399,769` exact MD5 duplicate images across splits, a `21.4%` false-match rate when joining crops by filename (`<session>_<crop>.jpg`), `57` byte-identical images labeled as both `Bottle` and `Jar`, `28` client shelf photos with burned-in red/green `cv2.rectangle` boxes, and `37.4%` `"Others"` catch-all labels. **Decision:** Enforce MD5 content-addressed joining, automated burned-in overlay quarantine, and `12` automated quality gates (`src/utils/qc.py`).
3. **Why Pure Embedding Search Hits an `82%–84%` Ceiling on Lookalike Variants (`EXP-05`, `EXP-06`):** `gemini-embedding-2` resolves **Brand (`96.7%`)**, **Category (`100%`)**, and **Packaging (`96.7%`)** in a single vector lookup at `₹0.011/image`. However, sister variants (*Dove Hair Fall Rescue* vs. *Daily Shine*; *Biolage Smoothproof* vs. *Scalpure*) share `85%` of their visual surface area, so global embeddings alone top out at `82.2%–84.4%` on exact SKU/variant. **Decision:** Use `gemini-embedding-2` (`Stage 2A`) to resolve the easy `68%` of crops immediately and shortlist the top-5 candidates for the hard `32%`.
4. **Why the 4-Crop Contact Sheet Unlocks `93.3%–100%` SKU Accuracy (`EXP-08`, `EXP-09`):** Sending a single composite image (**Contact Sheet**) containing the store crop + zoomed-in bottom-30% weight strip alongside the top-5 reference gallery images allows `Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite` / `3.8 Flash` to compare fine typography side-by-side, lifting exact SKU accuracy from `84.4%` to **`93.3%–100%`**.
5. **Why Grouping Identical Neighboring Packs Cuts Cost by `4x` (`EXP-14`):** On a real supermarket shelf with `25` facings, `4–5` identical packs sit side-by-side in a brand block. Grouping identical adjacent packs (`25` facings $\rightarrow$ `~6` unique product groups) cuts Stage 2B calls by `75%`, bringing total shelf cost down to **`₹0.12/shelf` (`₹1.18L / mo` per `1M` shelves)**.

## 3. Phase-2 Sequential Experiment Plan (`EXP-20` to `EXP-25`) — Checking Incrementality Step-by-Step

Our empirical category and packaging breakdown across `2,844` Unilever boxes showed that while **Beverages (`100%`)**, **Laundry (`97.0%`)**, **Home Hygiene (`95.8%`)**, **Hair Care (`95.5%`)**, and **Foods (`93.6%`)** already exceed `93%–100%`, three specific areas account for `>80%` of remaining errors:
1. **Squeeze Tubes (`77.0%` Variant/SKU accuracy)** inside **`Skin Care` (`81.7%`)** and **`Oral Care` (`80.8%`)** (*Biotique*, *Closeup*, *Pepsodent*, *Glow & Lovely*).
2. **Sideways-Stacked Soap Bars (`83.6%` Variant/SKU, `89.3%` Pack)** inside **`Personal Wash` (`80.7%`)** (*Ayush*, *Lux*, *Lifebuoy*).
3. **Exact Size / Grammage (`50g` vs. `100g` vs. `250g`)** when National `2,745`-SKU catalog distractors are unscoped.

To measure the **exact incremental gain ($\Delta$)** of each improvement, we executed **`EXP-20` through `EXP-26` sequentially** on the held-out `val` and `test` splits and logged all runs to `gs://ul-fs-s-sandbx-16-prj-shelf-images/results/`:
