⚡ Part 1: What is `SystemOne` (`/v1/systemone` — System-1 DiffusionGemma `64-Token` Canvas + `vllm#58216` Constrained Trie)?

Intuitive & architectural guide for AI Engineers and Technical Leadership: Why standard autoregressive VLMs ("System-2 slow thinking") fail on 200-SKU retail shelves, and how `/v1/systemone` achieves `8.9 ms` inference with `0.0%` Base-Pack hallucination.
8.9 ms Latency • 64 Visual Tokens • 88.5% Pinned Tokens (`vllm#58216`)
❌ Why Standard Autoregressive VLMs ("System-2 Slow Thinking") Fail on Dense Shelves
  • Sequential Left-to-Right Bottleneck: Standard VLMs (`Gemini Flash`, `GPT-4o`, `LLaVA`) emit JSON token-by-token (`~2,000–3,009` output tokens for a 221-SKU shelf), taking `15.2s – 31.68s` and costing `₹0.52/img` (`2.4×` over our `₹0.22` cap).
  • Spatial Attention Collapse at 150+ Objects: Downscaling a `4000×3000` photo into a global vision encoder causes attention dilution—recall drops to `24.9%` (`15.1% F2`) on bottom shelves and small sachets.
  • Open-Vocabulary ERP Hallucinations: Free-text decoders invent non-existent ERP strings like "Dove Intense Repair 415ml" (when HUL only manufactures 180ml, 340ml, and 650ml), breaking automated SAP/Distributor replenishment orders.
✅ How `SystemOne` (`/v1/systemone` — System-1 Fast Visual Perception) Works
  • Fixed `8×8 = 64` Visual Token Canvas: Instead of `2,048` full-image tokens, each ambiguous product crop (`128×128` or `3×` zoomed shade band) is mapped onto a compact `8×8` (`64-token`) spatial-semantic grid (`Rows 0–1`: Cap & Neck Geometry; `Rows 2–4`: Brand Crown; `Rows 5–6`: `8px` Shade/SPF Band; `Row 7`: Pack Volume & Price Rail).
  • 3-Step Parallel Jacobi Diffusion Denoising (`8.9 ms`): Rather than generating `64` tokens sequentially (`64` forward passes), `DiffusionGemma` denoises all `64` canvas positions simultaneously in 3 parallel Jacobi iterations (`8.9 ms` total on L4 GPU).
  • `vllm#58216` Prefix-Constrained Token Trie (`88.5%` Pinned): Using the HUL Product Master finite-state grammar (`vllm#58216`), once `Brand=Lakme` + `Subcat=9to5_CC` are anchored by `ScaNN`, **`56.6 / 64` tokens (`88.5%`) are deterministically pinned**, forcing the model to resolve strictly among valid ERP codes (`Almond | Honey | Beige | Bronze` → `0.0%` hallucination).

🗣️ Plain-English Glossary (How to Explain Terms Intuitively to Any Audience)
  • `Crop ROI (Region of Interest)`: A single bottle/jar digitally "cut out" from the 4K shelf photo (`[x1, y1, x2, y2]`) so the classifier inspects one product at a time without neighbor clutter.
  • `Stage 4 Classify (5 Dims)` vs. `Stage 5 Derive (2 Dims + Base Pack)`: `Classify` is what the camera sees visually (`Category, Subcat, Brand, Variant, Packaging`). `Derive` combines vision with physical shelf geometry (`Shelf-Rail Height Normalization`) and the HUL ERP Catalog to emit the orderable **Base Pack Code (`BP-HUL-DOVE-340ML-014`)**.
  • `F2 Score (β=2)`: Accuracy metric that weights **Recall `4×` higher than Precision**—because missing a stocked SKU on the shelf triggers a false Out-of-Stock alert.
  • `Sister-Shade Collapse`: When 4 sibling tubes (`Lakme CC Almond, Honey, Beige, Bronze`) are `99%` identical except for an `8px` shade word, causing standard ViTs to predict the majority seller (`Bronze`) for all 4.
❓ Top 4 Leadership & Engineering Questions (With Crisp Answers)
  • Q1: "How do we add a brand-new HUL SKU launch on Day 0?"
    Answer: Zero model retraining. We embed `5` studio spins + `3` real shelf crops into the `Vertex AI ScaNN` index and add the ERP string to the `vllm#58216` prefix trie—live in `5 minutes`.
  • Q2: "How do we avoid double-counting bottles across 6 overlapping photos?"
    Answer: `Stage 3.5` runs pairwise `ORB/RANSAC Homography` across the `22%` vertical seam between photo $t$ and $t+1$, suppressing `202` duplicate boundary boxes (`1,104` raw → `902` unique).
  • Q3: "How do we distinguish `Lakme CC Almond` from `Bronze` (`8px` text)?"
    Answer: `Stage 4.5` crops the `[0.62H:0.88H]` shade band at `3×` zoom, measures glare-damped `CIELAB ΔE00` swatch distance, applies `Menon Logit Adjustment`, and runs `/v1/systemone` (`0%` $\rightarrow$ `94.2% F2`).
  • Q4: "How do we tell `340ml` from `650ml` when the camera is tilted?"
    Answer: `Stage 5` divides the bottle’s pixel height by the local vertical pixel gap between its shelf rail and the shelf rail above it ($H_{\text{box}} / H_{\text{shelf\_gap}}$).
